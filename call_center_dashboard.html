Excel Workbook Architecture (`Call Center Dashboard.xlsx`)

Sheet: Dashboard
Master visual layout featuring interactive Excel Slicers, KPI cards, and dynamic Pivot Charts summarizing performance across rep and customer metrics.
Sheet: Data
Raw database table containing 1,000 cleansed call records with Customer ID, Duration, Purchase Amount, Representative, and Date fields.
Sheet: P1 (Top Customers)
Pivot Table aggregating total sales per customer. Led by C0005 ($7,747) and C0004 ($7,689) out of 15 key enterprise accounts.
Sheet: P2 (Representative Perf.)
Pivot Table analyzing sales revenue per sales representative. Representative R03 achieved top position with $20,872 (21.6% total).
Sheet: P3 (Monthly Trends)
Pivot Table tracking monthly revenue and call volume evolution from September to December 2010. Peak month: October ($33,338, 334 calls).
Sheet: P4 (Call Duration Buckets)
Pivot Table grouping call lengths into 30-second frequency intervals to identify agent efficiency and resolution speeds.
